Daily Programme

Each day brings unique rituals, cultural performances and community gatherings. All are welcome, entry is free.

10 OctoberSaturdayMahalaya

Mahalaya – Invoking the Goddess

The auspicious beginning of Durga Puja. Chandi Path recitation and sacred rituals invoke the Goddess's presence as we prepare the pandal.

Chandi PathRitual PreparationsBlessing Ceremony
16 OctoberFridayShashti

Shashti – Pandal Opening

The pandal officially opens! The idol of Goddess Durga is revealed for the first darshan. A joyful day of community gathering to honour the Goddess.

First DarshanPandal OpeningCommunity Gathering
17 OctoberSaturdaySaptami

Durga Saptami – Major Rituals

One of the most significant days. Major pujas and ceremonies fill the day with spiritual energy, alongside cultural programmes showcasing Bengali traditions.

Kshatri PujaCultural PerformancesMusic & Dance
18 OctoberSundayAshtami

Ashtami – Kumari & Sandhi Puja

Powerful rituals including Kumari Puja (worshipping young girls as the Goddess) and the solemn Sandhi Puja marking the transition to Navami.

Kumari PujaSandhi PujaEvening Celebrations
19 OctoberMondayNavami

Maha Navami – Grand Celebrations

The climax of the festival! Classical music, traditional dance, theatre and community feasts. A day of pure joy and togetherness.

Music ConcertsClassical DanceCommunity Feast
20 OctoberTuesdayDashami

Vijayadashami – Victory Day

The triumph of good over evil. Sindur Khela and Bijaya Sammilani, a joyful farewell to Goddess Durga with the whole community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is entry free?
Yes! Entry to the pandal is free for everyone. Voluntary contributions are welcomed to support the event.
What are the opening hours?
The pandal is generally open from morning through evening. Specific timings for rituals and cultural programmes will be shared on our invitations and social media.
Is it family-friendly?
Absolutely! Children of all ages are welcome. The festival is a wonderful opportunity to introduce cultural traditions to the next generation.
Can I volunteer or sponsor?
We'd love your support! Reach out at info@berlinerbengalis.com.
Is this only for Bengalis?
Not at all. Our celebration welcomes everyone, all backgrounds, faiths and cultures. Come experience and celebrate Bengali traditions with us!
Will food be available for all?
Maa Durga's offered Prasad will be distributed to everyone on a first-come, first-served basis. However, full meals are strictly reserved for registered members and active volunteers.
