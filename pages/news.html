Durga Puja 2026 - Announcement


Published: 2026-08-22

We are excited to publish the full programme for Durga Puja 2026. This year’s celebration will bring together classical music, dance, and youth workshops across ten days at Zwölf-Apostel-Kirchengemeinde. Please note that prepared meals will not be provided; a small fruit bhog/prasad will be offered after the main rituals on a first-come, first-served basis. Highlights include Sandhi Puja and a special children’s cultural workshop. Volunteers and sponsors are welcome - please contact info@berlinerbengalis.com for details.
