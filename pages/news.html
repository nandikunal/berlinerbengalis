Durga Puja 2026 — Full Programme Released

Published: 2026-08-10

We are excited to publish the full programme for Durga Puja 2026. This year’s celebration will bring together classical music, dance, youth workshops and community meals across ten days at Zwölf-Apostel-Kirchengemeinde. Highlights include Sandhi Puja, a special children’s cultural workshop and a community feast on Maha Navami. Volunteers and sponsors are welcome — please contact info@berlinerbengalis.com for details.

Environmental Initiative: Eco-friendly Pandal Materials

Published: 2026-05-02

Berliner Bengalis is committed to reducing festival waste. This year we are sourcing sustainable materials for our pandal decorations and promoting reusable serving ware during community meals. Our goal is to demonstrate that cultural traditions and environmental stewardship can go hand-in-hand.

Community Spotlight: Youth Cultural Exchange

Published: 2026-02-20

Our youth cultural exchange program connected young members with local schools and arts groups to present Bengali music and dance. The programme focused on intergenerational learning, language, and the role of tradition in modern urban life. We’ll expand these activities in 2026 with workshops and performance opportunities for young artists.

For press enquiries, partnerships or to volunteer, email info@berlinerbengalis.com.
