About Berliner Bengalis e.V.

Berliner Bengalis e.V. is a registered non-profit cultural association preserving and promoting Bengali culture and Hindu Sanatani traditions in Berlin.

Mission: Practice and promote traditions through cultural, religious and social activities.

Vision: Build a thriving Bengali community in Berlin that celebrates heritage and contributes positively to city life.
